1. Purpose

BioVerseAR is an educational tool for Grade 7 science. It lets students view 3D models in augmented reality, take quizzes, earn badges, and — optionally — compete on a class leaderboard. It is intended for use within your school's learning program.

2. Who can use it

It is for students and teachers of the school. Students under 18 use it as part of school activities under a teacher's supervision, with their parent's or guardian's permission.

3. Your account

Students sign in with a username and password — no email is required. You choose a cartoon explorer avatar, not a real photo. Keep your password private and do not share your account. You are responsible for activity under your account. If you forget your password, your teacher can reset it.

4. Acceptable use

Use BioVerseAR for learning. Please do not: cheat or help others cheat on quizzes; try to access another person's account or data; attempt to hack, overload, copy, or disrupt the app; or upload harmful or inappropriate content. Misuse may lead to your account being removed by your teacher.

Privacy Policy

What we collect

  • Username — shown to classmates on the leaderboard.
  • Full name — collected so your teacher can identify you in their class roster. It is private: only your own teacher can see it. Classmates never see it.
  • Explorer avatar — a cartoon character you pick. No real photo is ever collected.
  • Class code — if you join a competition, so you appear on your class board.
  • Quiz results & progress — your scores, badges, and answer times.

We do not collect your email, phone number, real-world location, or any photos.

How we use it

Only to run the app: to log you in, save your progress, show class leaderboards, and let your teacher see how their class is doing. We do not show ads and we never sell your information.

Who can see your data

  • Classmates — only your username, avatar, and score.
  • Your teacher — your full name and scores, for their own class only.
  • No one else — no other teacher or student can see your information.

How it's protected

Data is stored securely on Supabase with access rules so a student can only read their own data and a teacher can only read their own classes. Passwords are stored encrypted, never in plain text.

Your choices

You can change your avatar or password anytime in your Profile, and leave a competition to stop appearing on a leaderboard. A parent, guardian, or the student can ask the teacher or school to reset a password or remove the student's account and data.

Contact

For questions about these Terms or your data, please contact your teacher or the school.
